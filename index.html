Information We Collect Depending on how you use Florea, we may collect: - **Personal information:** Name, email address, phone number, and account details. - **Profile information:** Profile photos and other information you choose to provide. - **Location information:** Delivery address and location details you provide. Precise device location is accessed only if the relevant permission and feature are enabled. - **Product information:** Product photos, descriptions, prices, and seller information. - **Order information:** Shopping cart contents, order details, delivery information, and purchase history. - **Technical information:** Device information, application logs, and diagnostic data necessary to maintain the service. ## 3.
